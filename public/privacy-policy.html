§ 03 Data Storage & Firebase

All user data is stored and processed using Google Firebase infrastructure, which includes:

  • Firebase Authentication — Manages login sessions (Google OAuth + anonymous guest sessions)
  • Cloud Firestore — Stores user profiles, game statistics, leaderboard data, app configuration, purchases, coins, rewards, cosmetics, achievements, and pass progress
  • Firebase Realtime Database — Manages live game state, room data, player presence, and real-time events
  • Firebase Analytics — Helps us understand app usage and feature performance in aggregate
  • Firebase Crashlytics — Collects crash and diagnostic reports to help us fix bugs
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — Supports notifications where available and permitted

Online multiplayer may also use a Socket.IO game server to process room membership, player presence, match state, current turn data, card counts, and connection status. The server does not need your payment card details.

Firebase data may be stored on servers located in the United States or other countries where Google operates infrastructure. By using this app, you consent to cross-border data transfer consistent with Google's data processing policies.

We use Hive (a local NoSQL database) to store certain data on your device, including:

  • Cached profile and game settings
  • Offline game state snapshots
  • App preferences such as sound, theme, notification settings, and local gameplay cache

Local Hive data never leaves your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

Game Rules Version: 1.1.0  ·  Based on: Mattel Official UNO Rules

🃏
This section documents the official and house rules implemented in Tano. Administrators may enable or disable house rules for all rooms through the Admin Dashboard.

§ 01 Official Rules (Always Active)

  • Full 112-card deck (76 number + 24 action + 12 wild cards)
  • Each player is dealt 7 cards at game start
  • Match cards by color, number, or symbol
  • Wild cards can be played at any time
  • Wild Draw Four (+4) can only be played legally when you have no matching color card
  • Players must say "UNO" when they have one card remaining — penalty is 4 drawn cards if caught
  • First player to play all their cards wins the round
  • Scoring: number cards = face value; action cards = 20pts; wild cards = 50pts
  • First player to reach 500 points wins the game

§ 02 House Rules (Admin Configurable)

RuleDefaultDescription
Stack Draw CardsOFFPlay +2 on +2 or +4 on +4 to pass accumulated draws
7-0 RuleOFFPlaying 7 = swap hands; Playing 0 = rotate all hands
Draw Until PlayableONKeep drawing until you get a matching card
Jump-In RuleOFFPlay identical card out of turn sequence
Reverse = Skip (2-Player)ONIn 2-player games, Reverse acts as Skip
Mixed StackingOFFAllow +2 to be played on +4 in stacking mode
Mercy RuleOFFPlayer eliminated if hand exceeds 25 cards

§ 03 Disconnect & Fair Play Policy

  • If a player disconnects for any reason, they are marked as "quit" and their turn is automatically skipped
  • A disconnected player's cards are removed from the game
  • If only one active player remains after disconnections, they automatically win
  • Inactive rooms are automatically disposed: waiting rooms after 2 minutes, active games after 10 minutes
  • Attempting to manipulate disconnects to gain advantage is considered cheating and may result in a ban
